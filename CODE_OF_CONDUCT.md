# Code of Conduct

CareFlow contributors and maintainers are expected to keep collaboration respectful, constructive, and safe for people working in care, nonprofit, technical, and lived-experience contexts.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect across differences in background, identity, role, expertise, and language.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback about the work.
- Protect personal, health, and organization-confidential information.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, or publishing private communications.
- Correct mistakes openly and without retaliation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact or private security-reporting channel. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or escalate serious threats to the relevant platform or authorities.

Before the repository accepts public contributions, the team must designate an enforcement contact who is not the sole subject of a complaint.
